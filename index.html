The platform

One core. Many instruments.

The platform is where your matches live: video and data, synced and stored once. Instruments plug into it. They read from the same matches and write their work back, so a clip, a board and a drill can all point to the same moment.

The BNN Power Tools platform

Concept

Match video and event and tracking data, unified per match and kept as one growing record. Every event links to its moment in the video, and every clip carries its data.

The instruments

One instrument, one job, done properly.

Each instrument runs on the platform and shares its matches. Every one belongs to a family and carries a catalogue code that never changes.

NoteNothing here is released yet. The platform and all five instruments are concepts we are designing and testing. Their names are working titles and may change.

Concept

VID·01 · Video

VideoFX

Event data, drawn onto match video. Pick events from the platform and VideoFX finds the moments, then turns them into clips and overlays.

DAT·01 · Data

DataMorph

Your data, accumulating match by match. DataMorph brings in event and tracking data from every match and grows it into one consistent record, so trends and comparisons are there when you need them.

DataConcept

VIS·01 · Visualisation

PitchScope

Tactics boards for coaches and analysts. Set up shapes and movements on a geometrically correct pitch, starting from real positions in your matches.

Data layers for the tactics board. Stack heatmaps, passing lanes, pitch control and tracking data on a PitchScope board, one layer at a time.

How we build

Built from real matches.

01

Based on actual matches

Every instrument starts from real match video and data, not idealised examples or textbook diagrams.

02

Works in practice over theory

If it doesn't help in the team meeting or on the training pitch, it doesn't ship. Useful beats elegant.

03

Continuous evolution

Instruments are released early, labelled honestly and improved match by match.

04

Video and data, unified

Video and data live together on the platform. Every event links to its moment, and every clip carries its data.
