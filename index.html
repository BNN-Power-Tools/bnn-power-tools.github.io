Match video and data, together.

Football analysis deserves better tools, and ours start from one idea: video and data belong in the same place. The platform keeps every match's video and data together, and focused instruments do one job each on top of it, from clipping and telestration to tracking models and tactics boards built from real match situations.

The platform

One match. Video and data together.

The platform is where your matches live, with video and data synced and stored together. Instruments plug into it, read the same matches and write their work back, so a clip, a data overlay and a tactics board all point to the same moment.

The instruments

One instrument, one job, done properly.

Each instrument works on video and data together, straight from the platform. Every one belongs to a family and carries a catalogue code that never changes.

How we build

Built from real matches.

01

Video and data, together

Video and data are never split into separate tools. Every event links to its moment, and every clip carries its data.

02

Based on actual matches

Every instrument starts from real match video and data, not idealised examples or textbook diagrams.

03

Works in practice over theory

If it doesn't help in the team meeting or on the training pitch, it doesn't ship. Useful beats elegant.

04

Continuous evolution

Instruments are released early, labelled honestly and improved match by match.
