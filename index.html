Football analysis deserves better tools.

A growing collection of focused instruments for football video, data, visualisation and spatial analysis. Each tool does one job, has a character of its own, and is honest about whether it's a concept, an experiment or a release.

How we build

Instruments, not platforms.

01

One tool, one job

Each tool does a job you could name in one sentence, and does it properly.

02

Show the working

Methods, data sources and limitations are documented, not hidden.

03

Built on the pitch

Everything is drawn on football's real geometry: 105 × 68, the 9.15 m circle, the 16.5 m box.

04

Honest labels

Every tool says whether it's a concept, an experiment or a release. When in doubt, the less mature label wins.

Reading the labels

The badge fills up as a tool matures.

ConceptAn idea with design work. No usable software yet.
ExperimentA working prototype. It may change or disappear.
PreviewUsable by invitation. Features may change.
BetaPublic and mostly stable, with known gaps.
ReleasedSupported, versioned and documented.
ArchivedNo longer maintained. Kept for reference.
